# Rooftop Rush — data and ads

Document version: 20 November 2026.

Developer: Maria Tameeva.

Contact: winslowmaria044@gmail.com.

The game needs no account. Levels, stars, records, coins, purchases in the game's shop, tasks, language and settings are kept on the device. There is no cloud transfer between devices. Clearing the app's data or removing the app removes this progress.

## Optional ads

Ads are shown only when the player chooses one: a second chance in a run, the run's coins ×2, a new try of a level with a pot, and a free chest once a day. They come through the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. It starts when an ad is requested, and only after the player allows ads to load. Without that permission the game plays as usual.

When loading and showing an ad, Yandex may process the IP address, device, operating system and app parameters, service identifiers, and data about the connection, impressions, clicks and technical errors. The purposes are ad delivery and measurement, granting the reward, diagnostics and protection against invalid impressions. An approximate region may be derived from the IP address.

In this app, ad personalisation and the SDK's location tracking are off, and the age-restricted ad mode is on. No permissions for location, contacts, camera or microphone are requested. The Android Advertising ID is not requested. These settings do not mean that the ad network receives no technical data at all.

Yandex privacy policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/ — it describes how Yandex processes and stores the data it receives and how to contact it. The game's developer does not receive a copy of any ad profile. If you write to support, the developer receives your address and your message in order to reply.

## Control

Settings → Ads and data → Withdraw permission stops new ad requests until permission is given again. It does not delete data Yandex has already processed. For questions about the game and data, write to the support address; for the ad network's data, see the contact options in the Yandex policy.

Test builds show test ads. There are no purchases for money and no subscriptions.
